What whole life insurance is (and isn't)

Whole life insurance is a form of permanent life insurance: when premiums are paid as required, the death benefit is designed to last your lifetime. Any guarantees are the ones written into your contract and are subject to the claims-paying ability of the issuing insurer. Many policies build cash value over time with both guaranteed and non-guaranteed elements; details vary by insurer and product. Nothing here is tax or legal advice. Analiza reviews illustrations and policy documents with you line by line.

Why families ask about whole life

People often look at whole life when they want coverage that does not end after a set term, a premium schedule set out in the contract, or a policy that builds cash value while protection stays in place. Analiza compares those goals with alternatives, such as term insurance paired with other savings, so you choose with context, not pressure.

Whole life vs term: the short version

Term life covers a set period and generally costs less at the start; whole life is designed to last for life and generally costs more at the start. Which fits depends on how long you need coverage, your budget and what else you want the policy to do. The full comparison, with Virginia consumer protections such as the 10-day free look, is in the guide Whole Life vs Term Life Insurance in Virginia.

How cash value works

Cash value is the amount the insurer would pay you if you surrendered the policy, and it can also back a policy loan. It tends to build slowly in the early years, and loans or surrenders have trade-offs for your coverage and possibly your taxes. The guide How Whole Life Cash Value Works walks through each one with clearly labelled examples.

Whole life insurance questions

What is whole life insurance?

Permanent coverage with a premium schedule set by the contract that may build cash value as defined in your policy. It is designed to provide lifetime protection when you keep the contract in good standing.

How is whole life different from term life insurance?

Term covers a fixed period at a typically lower starting cost; whole life is designed for lifetime coverage and includes contract-defined cash-value features. See the full comparison.
