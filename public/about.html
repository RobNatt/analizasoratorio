About Analiza Soratorio

Analiza Soratorio is a life insurance agent with World Financial Group, licensed in Virginia and based in Chesapeake. She helps families and business owners understand whole life insurance, term life insurance and annuities, and choose coverage they can explain to the people who depend on them.

Who Analiza works with

Most conversations start with a change in someone's life: a new home and mortgage, a new baby, a business that now has partners or employees, or a retirement date coming into view. Analiza works with households and business owners across Virginia by phone and video, so you can meet from your kitchen table or your office. Consultations are free, and a review of the coverage you already have costs nothing.

Her approach

The goal is simple: you should know what you own, what it costs and what happens in the "what if" scenarios before you sign anything. In practice that means:

  • Goals before products. The first question is what you want the coverage to do, whether that is paying off a mortgage, replacing income for a set number of years or leaving something behind for the next generation.
  • Plain language. Terms such as cash value, dividends, riders and surrender charges are explained in everyday words.
  • Illustrations reviewed line by line. You'll see which values are guaranteed by the contract (subject to the insurer's claims-paying ability) and which are not.
  • Alternatives on the table. Whole life is compared with term life and with term paired with other savings, so you can choose with context, not pressure.
  • Ongoing reviews. Promotions, relocations and births all change what your coverage should do, so periodic check-ins are part of the relationship.

What Analiza helps with

  • Whole life insurance: permanent coverage designed to last for life, with cash value.
  • Term life insurance: coverage for a set number of years, often sized around a mortgage or the years children are at home.
  • Indexed and fixed annuities: what the contract does and does not guarantee, and what the fees and riders mean.
  • Policy ownership and beneficiary designations, coordinated with the will or trust your own attorney prepares.

Analiza does not give tax, legal or estate-planning advice. When a question crosses into those areas, she'll suggest you bring in your own tax or legal professional.
