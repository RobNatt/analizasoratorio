Services

How Analiza can help

Analiza works with households and business owners by phone and video, with clear, practical explanations of how each type of coverage works. Your coverage should keep pace as your life changes.

Term & permanent life

Death-benefit options sized around mortgages, dependents and business obligations, explained in plain terms. Start with the whole life insurance overview.

Indexed & fixed annuities

How annuity contracts work, what is and is not guaranteed by the contract, and how riders and fees affect the result, walked through before you decide.

Insurance for retirement years

A look at how life insurance and annuity income might sit alongside the other income you expect in retirement. For tax questions, Analiza will point you to your own tax professional.

Owners & beneficiaries

Approach

How Analiza works with clients

  • Goals first. Conversations start with what you want the coverage to do, before any product is discussed.
  • Plain language. You'll know what you own, what it costs and what happens in "what if" scenarios. Illustrations are reviewed line by line, including which values are guaranteed and which are not.
  • Based in Chesapeake. Virtual consultations and flexible appointment times, including evenings, are available to fit your schedule.
  • Ongoing reviews. Promotions, relocations, births: your policies should reflect real life, so check-ins are part of the process.
